## Libraries opened at run time (D036)

Measured with `LD_DEBUG=libs` while the app ran under WSLg with its window shown. Avalonia,
.NET and Tmds.DBus open these with `dlopen`, so dpkg-shlibdeps cannot see them:
libX11, libXext, libXfixes, libXi, libXrandr, libICE, libSM, libdbus-1 and ICU 74.
fontconfig is linked by libSkiaSharp.so and found by dpkg-shlibdeps.

`scripts/deb-check.sh` now repeats this measurement on every run: in its clean container the
app runs with `LD_DEBUG=files`, and each library the program or its two libraries asked dlopen
for is looked up with `dpkg -S`. Last run: libICE, libSM, libX11, libXfixes, libXi, libXrandr,
libdbus-1, libicuuc and libicui18n 74, all 9 in Depends. libXext is loaded as a dependency of
libXi and libXrandr rather than opened directly; declaring it is harmless.
